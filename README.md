This add-on lets you switch cameras and build rough edits quickly inside Blender.
Press J to open a Pie Menu of cameras. Choosing a camera sets it as the active camera and drops a camera-linked marker on the current frame,
letting you experiment with cuts in real time during playback.

Use the N-Panel → Multicam to assign specific cameras to specific pie-menu wedge positions (Left, Right, Top, Bottom, and diagonal slots). Unassigned slots remain blank.
This gives you full control over where each camera appears in the Pie Menu, instead of relying on alphabetical order.

Markers added by the Pie Menu switch cameras automatically during playback and rendering.
This makes it easy to build a rough cut of your scene straight from the timeline and export a sequence with all chosen shots already in place.

Ideal for multi-camera setups such as dialogue scenes, interviews, previs, or any long sequence where fast camera testing is helpful.

Key features:
	•	J key opens a customizable camera Pie Menu
	•	Selecting a camera sets it active + adds a linked marker
	•	N-Panel interface for assigning camera positions
	•	Blank wedges for unused slots
	•	Automatically switches cameras during playback and render

Install:
Download the script → Blender Preferences → Add-ons → Install → enable “Multicam Quick Editor.”

Usage:
Assign cameras in the N-Panel, press J, choose your shot, and play back your scene to evaluate your cuts.
